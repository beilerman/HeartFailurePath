## How the three "What matters most" cases were chosen

Cases 45, 29 and 44 are one patient, shown with "What matters most" set to Balanced, Keep cost low and Keep the regimen simple. An earlier pair used a patient for whom all three settings gave the same first option, which showed nothing; this patient was found by searching for one (a man of 71 on an ACE inhibitor, a beta-blocker and spironolactone, on Medicare with a $300 monthly budget, worried about cost and able to manage few pills) for whom each of the two settings gives a first option different from Balanced.

- Balanced (case 45): Stop Lisinopril, wait 36 hours, then start Sacubitril/Valsartan; Add Dapagliflozin 10mg once daily
- Keep cost low (case 29): Add Dapagliflozin 10mg once daily; Titrate Carvedilol from 12.5mg twice daily to 25mg twice daily
- Keep the regimen simple (case 44): Add Dapagliflozin 10mg once daily; Titrate Carvedilol from 12.5mg twice daily to 25mg twice daily
