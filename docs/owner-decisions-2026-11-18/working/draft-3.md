Inputs: draft-1, three round-1 reviews, sensitivity.md, draft-2, round2-calculations.md, round2-challenge.md (same folder). Each item: ANSWER; confidence; effect; what remains open.

1. **Starts and increases.** A start is valued as titrated (trial intention-to-treat), but never beyond the highest dose the engine's own kidney, interaction and maximum-dose rules allow this patient; an increase is valued at the dose it reaches; a held drug by its dose. Confidence high. Effect: 9 first options. Open: a drug the patient already takes is undervalued relative to a start (item 15).

2. **Weights.** OWNER'S VALUE JUDGMENT. Recommendation: keep today's numbers for the case review, labelled policy; commission sourced utilities once for both engines; do not adopt the utility-derived weights as computed. Facts: derived weights (reviewer's assumed utilities) change 203 first options (20%), lose all three guideline targets now met, and are most sensitive to the unsourced disutility of an adverse event; the hospitalization weight is the least defensible number (today's weights imply about 3.4 quality-adjusted years lost per admission against a sourced 0.01 to 0.15) but hardly changes first options; the side-effect weight is what moves them. Sourced: hospitalization (Goehler 2009; SHIFT EQ-5D model) and KCCQ-to-EQ-5D mappings exist; none found for life expectancy or adverse events. Confidence in "do not change now": high. Confidence that the present ratios are right: low.

3. **Side-effect scale.** Keep the 0.5 / 1 / 3 severity weights (shared with the hypertension engine). The divisor of 2 is a weight, not a clamp fix: say so and correct the stale description in data/harmDefinitions.json. The value of one burden point (0.07 here, 0.22 in the hypertension engine) is settled with item 2. Confidence high. Effect: none now.

4. **Ten upper-bound harm rows.** Replace the worst case by the average excess across the classes where that harm was measured, labelled "class average, not measured for this drug"; hypomagnesemia needs a diuretic-specific source (the present bound is patiromer's own 4-week rate). ONE RULING FOR BOTH ENGINES (it is also an open decision in the hypertension engine). Confidence medium. Effect: 1 to 11 first options.

5. **Precision weighting.** Keep, centred on no effect, no exception; use a separate prior for death (0.030) and for hospitalization (0.094). Confidence: method high; separate priors medium (bootstrap and permutation P about 0.01, likelihood-ratio P about 0.10; mostly a difference in mean effect size). Effect: 55 first options. OWNER TO CONFIRM his Cochrane ruling: published 0.24 (death) and 0.07 (admission); used today 0.83 and 0.86; with separate priors 0.90 and 0.80.

6. **New classes per visit.** Keep the default at 2 and keep counting IV iron and potassium binders until after the case review. Confidence high. The option generator never builds "switch + two additions" although the limit allows it: this should be fixed, but it changes 46 first options, so the owner should see examples first.

7. **Finerenone at LVEF 41 to 49.** Keep displayed; the three guideline targets stay counted as not reproduced, annotated. Confidence high (trial and FDA indication verified).

8. **KCCQ anchor.** Keep 20 points. Confidence medium. Open: additivity across classes and double charging of symptom-type side effects are untested.

9. **Low-dose credit.** Keep 0.364 (20 to 22 first options across its measured range). The curve is anchored on the label starting dose, so spironolactone 25 mg earns 0.364 although the RALES dose was 26 mg; re-anchor on the fraction of the trial dose, only for drugs with their own trial-dose anchor. OWNER TO SEE FIRST: with spironolactone 25 mg credited at 0.96, 74 first options change and in 41 the tool lowers spironolactone from 50 to 25 mg and adds vericiguat. Paired ruling needed: should the tool ever lower a tolerated dose because the higher dose has no measured extra benefit. Recommendation: fix the credit, and do not offer an unforced reduction of a tolerated drug (no trial tested deliberate down-titration; the harm rates are for starting a drug, not for a patient who already tolerates it). Confidence: defect real, high; remedy, medium.

10. **Fixed safety penalties.** Keep all of them as labelled policy for now. The four recorded-value bands change no displayed order in the reference runs but do in 39 of 6,000 random patients, and selection code reads them, so they are not provably inert. Separate finding: the per-option warning penalty is the only thing keeping ivabradine from being added to a beta-blocker (96 more first options), although SHIFT tested ivabradine on top of beta-blockers and the bradycardia is already in the harm table; about a third of the warnings it penalizes are procedural notes (digoxin level monitoring). Recommendation after the case review: stop penalizing procedural notes and harms the table already charges. The hypotension-by-systolic-pressure modifier is not ready (PARADIGM-HF interaction P = 0.21; only part of the engine's row is resolved by pressure). Confidence: keep-for-now high.

11. **Bonus for replacing a contraindicated drug.** Delete it, after the case review and with examples shown. It changes nothing in the reference runs but changes the first option in 15 of 6,000 random patients: it prefers replacing within the same class over adding a different class. Without it that choice is made by measured effects. Confidence medium. No new eligibility rule.

12. **Beta-blockers in atrial fibrillation.** Add a condition on CURRENT rhythm. Death value = the engine's own beta-blocker figure (0.66) times the measured atrial-fibrillation difference after precision weighting (ratio about 1.25), about 0.82; plausible range 0.77 to 0.88. Never a reason to stop: no displayed option newly removes or reduces a beta-blocker. Effect: 24 to 31 first options, all beta-blocker starts displaced by another drug; insensitive to the exact value. Hospitalization in atrial fibrillation is unresolved: the only rhythm-specific figures are cardiovascular (not heart failure) hospitalization in Cleland 2018; the owner reading Kotecha 2014 in full would settle it, and the unconfirmed CIBIS-II 0.64. Confidence: condition high; number medium.
