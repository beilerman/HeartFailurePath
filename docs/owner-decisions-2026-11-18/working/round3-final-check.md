## 2. Item 9: is the reduction forced or chosen?

Not a reduction at all (see 9 above). Base engine: unforced dose reductions are generated only for (i) a safety reason: heart rate below the drug's threshold, potassium 5.5 to 5.9 on an MRA, a renal or interaction cap exceeded, a beta-blocker with acute hypoperfusion (`reduceReason`, `isBBForcedDown`), or (ii) a diuretic in a patient with no congestion finding (`reductionIndicated`, `candidates.ts` about line 275). A reduction of a tolerated non-diuretic drug for score reasons is not a candidate ("unquantified loss of benefit cannot justify reducing established therapy"). Two concrete patients where the floor changes the lead:
- "NYHA II + BNP 1800 (Vericiguat Eligible)" (commercial): on spironolactone 25. Base lead: titrate spironolactone 25 to 50 (score 76). With the floor: add vericiguat 5 mg (77). Spironolactone is unchanged.

Patients where the base does generate an unforced reduction: (1) euvolemic patient at dry weight on furosemide 40 mg with no edema, JVP or crackles (diuretic de-escalation); (2) any patient with heart rate 52 on carvedilol 25 mg bid (forced one-step reduction), or spironolactone 50 at eGFR 42 (cap 25 mg).

## 4. Item 12: the 0.66

Row `beta-blocker-reduced-death`: rr 0.66 (0.58 to 0.74), basis meta_analysis, fixed-effect inverse-variance pool of CIBIS-II 0.66, MERIT-HF 0.66, COPERNICUS 0.65 (pooled ln -0.4202, se 0.0624). Populations LVEF <=35 / <=40 / <25, NYHA II-IV, on ACE inhibitor and diuretic; rhythm not restricted (these trials enrolled patients with and without atrial fibrillation; the share is not stated in the row; Kotecha's 17% is the IPD figure). The row's note says the sinus-rhythm-only IPD pool of the same class is 0.73 (0.67 to 0.80), weaker because it includes bucindolol and nebivolol.
Is "0.66 x shrunk AF-to-sinus ratio" coherent? No. The ratio is AF versus sinus; 0.66 is an all-rhythm pool of three drugs. Two coherent routes: (1) take BOTH rhythm rows from one source (Kotecha 2014 / Beta-Blockers in Heart Failure Collaborative Group: sinus 0.73, AF 0.91 after shrinkage), which lowers the non-AF benefit from 0.66 to 0.73 and changes every non-AF patient; or (2) decompose the engine's all-rhythm 0.66: with AF share p = 0.17 and ratio 1.249, ln sinus = ln 0.66 - p x 0.222 gives sinus 0.64, ln AF = ln 0.66 + (1-p) x 0.222 gives AF 0.79, and both rows change (sinus slightly stronger). Draft-3's 0.82 leaves the sinus row at 0.66 and the all-rhythm average then no longer equals 0.66. The difference (0.82 versus 0.79) is small, but the statement "from the engine's own 0.66" should become "from one source for both rows", and the result is a labelled extrapolation.
