## Corrections to implement (CONFIRMED / PARTLY CONFIRMED only, most important first)

1. GLP-1 KCCQ must be placebo-adjusted: semaglutide 2.4 mg = 7.8 (7.3 with T2DM); tirzepatide 15 mg = 6.9; lower doses scale from these.
2. Vericiguat NT-proBNP initiation gate: >=1000 pg/mL in sinus rhythm, >=1600 in AF (BNP >=300 / >=500). Add an advisory (not a block) at NT-proBNP >8000.
3. Vericiguat interaction gates: contraindicate riociguat; block or strongly warn on PDE5 inhibitors; warn on long-acting nitrate co-prescription (excluded in VICTORIA).
4. Empagliflozin initiation gate: block only if eGFR <20 (dapagliflozin stays at <25 for initiation; continuation allowed below).
5. H/ISDN Class 1 rule: require background ACEi/ARB/ARNI and beta-blocker (or documented intolerance), Black, NYHA III-IV, HFrEF. Class 2b rule: HFrEF unable to take RAAS inhibitors for intolerance/renal insufficiency.
6. Iron (FCM): restrict feature credit and rescue to LVEF <50 (full at <=40); replace single 1000 mg tier with the label weight x Hb table plus 500 mg maintenance at 12/24/36 weeks; add Hb input (hold at >=15).
7. Iron (FCM) safety and cost: add boxed-warning monitoring (phosphate before repeat dosing); hypophosphatemia rate about 27%; cost about $1,121 per 1000 mg (Medicare coinsurance about $224).
8. GLP-1 initiation LVEF: semaglutide >=45, tirzepatide >=50, both BMI >=30; no initiation at LVEF 41-44.
9. GLP-1 titration: add semaglutide 0.5 and 1.7 mg, tirzepatide 7.5 and 12.5 mg; titrate_up one tier at a time, minimum 4 weeks per step.
10. Tirzepatide dose-weight map: 5 mg = 0.67-0.72 and 10 mg = 0.92-0.93 of 15 mg effect; reduce weight effect in T2DM (semaglutide -6.4 vs -10.7 points; tirzepatide -10.4% vs -12.9%).

## Additional findings not in the original review

- Vericiguat label adverse reactions in VICTORIA: hypotension 16% (placebo 15%), anemia 10% (placebo 7%); NEJM symptomatic hypotension 9.1% vs 7.9%. The engine values quoted in the review (10% and 5%) are lower than the label's.
- Vericiguat: the label default starting dose (5 mg) differs from the VICTORIA start (2.5 mg), and VICTORIA excluded long-acting nitrates, which matters when H/ISDN is also recommended.
- Injectafer boxed warning (hypophosphatemia) is new in the label revision of 08/2026.
- Wegovy label now includes oral tablets and a 1.7 mg maintenance option; HF efficacy evidence exists for injection 2.4 mg only.
- Part D Bridge coverage is for BMI-eligible patients with prior authorization, and the $50 copay does not count toward the out-of-pocket cap.
