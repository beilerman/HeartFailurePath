# Ranking change: 2026 ESC phenotype (HFmrEF removed), 2026-11-11

Produced by `npm run ranking:diff` against the golden snapshot before the update, over the 258 scenarios that existed before this change
(774 runs = 258 x 3 insurance tiers). Two scenarios were renamed and nine added in the same commit; they are not in this comparison.

Summary: top pick changed in 31 of 774 runs, all with baseline LVEF 50 or higher (HFpEF now has MRA as a Class I foundational therapy,
so the guideline domain no longer saturates at 100 on SGLT2i alone). 0 of 42 runs at LVEF 41-49 and 0 of 639 runs at LVEF 40 or lower changed
their top pick (their guideline-domain table changed, the leading regimens did not). The three alert differences are the missing-LVEF alert
text (HFmrEF wording removed).

Clinical read of the 31 changes: all are HFpEF patients (LVEF 58) on or near SGLT2i. (1) Obese HFpEF de novo now leads with SGLT2i + MRA
(finerenone, or spironolactone under Medicare) instead of SGLT2i + tirzepatide: MRA is Class I in HFpEF, the GLP-1/GIP agonist is Class IIa for
symptoms. (2) The "Adjunct-Source" obese HFpEF scenarios (current regimen dapagliflozin) now lead with SGLT2i + MRA + semaglutide (two new classes, within
the visit limit of two) instead of SGLT2i + MRA, because the removed saturation lets the completeness domain count the GLP-1 slot.
(3) Dose-step ties (semaglutide 1.7 to 2.4, 0.5 to 1, tirzepatide 10 to 12.5, spironolactone 12.5 to 25) flip by under 0.6 raw points.
No pillar is lost and no drug starts outside its evidence range.
