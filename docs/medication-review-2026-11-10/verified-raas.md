## Direct answers to the required questions

1. Entresto label: standard start 49/51 bid (not 24/26). The start is halved to 24/26 bid if the patient is not taking an ACEi/ARB or took a low dose; if eGFR <30; and with moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B). Mild or moderate renal impairment: no change. Child-Pugh C: not recommended. Washout: 36 h. Contraindications: hypersensitivity; prior ACEi/ARB angioedema; concomitant ACEi; aliskiren in diabetes. SBP: no initiation threshold in the label; hypotension warning says correct volume/salt depletion or start lower. PARADIGM excluded SBP <100.
2. Table 14 (secondary copy): captopril 6.25 TID to 50 TID; enalapril 2.5 bid to 10-20 bid; lisinopril 2.5-5 to 20-40 daily; ramipril 1.25-2.5 to 10 daily; candesartan 4-8 to 32 daily; losartan 25-50 to 50-150 daily; valsartan (start 20-40) to 160 bid; ARNI 49/51 bid (may start 24/26) to 97/103 bid.
3. Generic sacubitril/valsartan launched in the US 22-24 Jul 2025 (Novadoz/MSN, Torrent). Medicare negotiated price $295 per 30 days, effective 1 Jan 2026 (list WAC $628).
4. Losartan 150 mg tablet does not exist (25/50/100 only). Captopril 12.5 mg does exist (12.5/25/50/100).
5. Enalapril: see E2 (HTN: CrCl <=30 start 2.5 mg, max 40/day; HF: Cr >1.6 or Na <130 start 2.5 mg daily, titrate to max 40/day). Lisinopril: see L3 (CrCl 10-30 halve initial dose, titrate to max 40; <10 or HD 2.5 mg).

## Corrections to implement (CONFIRMED / PARTLY CONFIRMED only, most important first)

1. ARNI start-tier rule on `add`: use 24/26 bid when the patient is not on an ACEi/ARB, is on a low dose of one, eGFR <30, or Child-Pugh B. Otherwise start at 49/51 bid. Never add at 97/103. Reach 97/103 only via titrate_up, with at least 2 wk between steps. (S1, S2)
2. Apply the same "add at lowest tier" rule to ACEi/ARB adds (label start doses: lisinopril 5 mg HF, enalapril 2.5 mg, ramipril 1.25-2.5 mg, captopril 6.25-25 mg TID, valsartan 40 mg bid, candesartan 4 mg). (S1 generalization, label HF sections)
3. Child-Pugh B must not exclude ARNI: reduced start (24/26) plus a caution. Child-Pugh C stays excluded, labeled "not recommended / not studied". (S3)
4. Add an aliskiren contraindication for ARNI, ACEi and ARB when the patient has diabetes (Entresto and Zestril labels). (S8)
5. Hard-gate ARNI + ACEi, and require a 36 h gap when switching in either direction. (S11)
6. Pricing: Entresto `generic_available: true`; generic cash ~$30-75 per 30 d with a discount card, brand/no-card cash $600-750; Medicare negotiated ceiling $295/30 d from 2026. (S4)
7. Losartan: flag 150 mg as an additional target dose (Table 14 target 50-150 mg); the 100 mg flag stays. Relabel 150 mg as two tablets (100 + 50) because no 150 mg tablet exists. (LO1, LO2)
8. Remove renal max_dose caps that exceed what the label requires: lisinopril (label: start 2.5 mg at CrCl 10-30, titrate to 40), enalapril (HF: Cr >1.6 or Na <130 start 2.5 mg, titrate to 40 mg/day), losartan (no renal adjustment), candesartan (no renal-specific adjustment). Keep valsartan 80 mg at <30 (safety not established <30) and ramipril (label max 5 mg/day at CrCl <40 for HF). (L3, E2, LO3, CA2, V2, R2)
9. Add missing tablet strengths: captopril 12.5 mg; lisinopril 30 mg. (C1, L1)
10. Add a caution (not a block) for ARNI initiation at baseline SBP <100 (PARADIGM excluded <100). Do not cite PIONEER-HF for an SBP cutoff. (S9)
11. Display-only AE rates for ARNI: hypotension 18%, hyperkalemia 12%, cough 9% per the label table. Comment "Category X" becomes "boxed warning: fetal toxicity". (S10, S7)
12. Add an ACEi/ARB bid option for ramipril (2.5 bid to 5 bid is the label's post-MI HF regimen). (R1)

Not verified, leave as is: LVEF/NT-proBNP/KCCQ magnitudes (S5, S6), PIONEER-HF SBP criterion (S9b), side-effect rates other than the ARNI hypotension/hyperkalemia/cough figures, and the retail price of the other generics ($4-$10 values).
