# Verified clinical decisions, 2026-11-11

## Method note

Research only; no code changed. Each claim was checked against sources opened in this session. Routes that worked: DailyMed SPL XML (labels), PubMed E-utilities abstracts, ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, and Europe PMC open-access full text. ahajournals.org, jacc.org, academic.oup.com, onlinelibrary.wiley.com, escardio.org, kidney-international.org and professional.heart.org returned 403 (or timed out) to automated fetch. No full text of the 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA guideline, the 2021 ESC guideline (or its practical-guidance web tables), the 2023 ESC focused update, the 2021/2024 ACC ECDPs, the 2018 ESC pregnancy guideline, the KDIGO 2020 conference report, or the JACC 2020 diuretic review could be opened. Guideline wording below therefore comes from open-access secondary reproductions (multi-society or European consensus papers, peer-reviewed reviews) and is labelled "secondary"; it should be re-checked against the primary guideline before being cited as guideline text. Verdicts: CONFIRMED = a source was opened and a supporting sentence (25 words or fewer) is quoted; CONFIRMED (secondary) = the claim is about guideline text but only a secondary reproduction was opened; NOT VERIFIED = not found in opened sources; REFUTED = an opened source says otherwise. Nothing is stated from memory unless tagged "from memory, not verified". A secondary summary page (Guideline Central, published 2026-09-01) says the ESC issued a new 2026 heart failure guideline that replaces 2021; I did not open the guideline itself, so 2021 ESC-derived thresholds below may be superseded. A web-search summary (not opened by me) also attributed a Class 2b B-R binder recommendation and an MRA "discontinue if K cannot be kept below 5.5" sentence to the 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA guideline; treat that as a lead only.

## What these sources support

**A. MRA initiation and potassium.** Most defensible guideline-concordant rule: start an MRA in HFrEF (NYHA II-IV) when K <5.0 mEq/L and eGFR >30 (COR 1/LOE A, 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA, via secondary reproduction only). Labels are not uniform and should not be assumed equal to the guideline: spironolactone says K <=5.0 and eGFR >50; eplerenone says contraindicated if K >5.5 at initiation or CrCl <=30; finerenone says do not start if K >5.0 (and its HF indication is LVEF >=40%). Monitoring: labels say K within 1 week (spironolactone, eplerenone) plus 1 month (eplerenone) or 4 weeks (finerenone); a secondary guideline reproduction gives roughly 1 week, 4 weeks, then every 6 months; ACEi/ARNI checks at 1-2 weeks after start or titration (secondary). In-use thresholds: halve MRA at K >5.5, withhold at K >6.0 (secondary ESC-derived consensus; ESC primary table not opened). Potassium binders: Class 2b, B-R in 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA (secondary). DIAMOND supports starting or continuing an MRA concurrently with patiromer in patients with current hyperkalemia (K >5.0) or past hyperkalemia within 12 months, but only inside a trial run-in with MRA titrated toward 50 mg; it is not a guideline instruction to initiate an MRA at K >5.0. No numeric recency window for the baseline K/creatinine was found anywhere. Strength: label and trial primary; guideline secondary only.

**B. Hyperkalemia severity and acuity.** Not verified against KDIGO. A secondary review uses mild 5.1-5.5, moderate 5.6-6.0, severe >6.0 and advises stopping or reducing RAASi plus a binder at >6.0; this conflicts in cutoffs with the KDIGO scheme the question describes, so no single severity classification can be asserted from opened sources. ESC ACEi/ARB/ARNI halve/stop thresholds were not found (only MRA thresholds). Strength: secondary only / not verified.

**C. Beta-blockers and heart rate.** Only label statements are verified: carvedilol "reduce dose if pulse below 55"; metoprolol succinate (generic ER label) and bisoprolol (generic hypertension label) give no numeric heart rate, only "symptomatic bradycardia" (reduce, metoprolol) and contraindication in severe/marked sinus bradycardia. The ESC "<50 bpm halve dose" rule is not verified. A 2025 European consensus gives a resting heart-rate goal of 60-69 per minute, not a halving rule. Strength: label for numbers; ESC rule not verified.

**D. Acuity and pregnancy.** Hospitalization/urgent-evaluation wording: not verified. Pregnancy: a 2024 peer-reviewed review states ACEi/ARNI/MRA contraindicated, SGLT2i/ivabradine/vericiguat not recommended, metoprolol preferred (atenolol avoided), hydralazine plus nitrates as the substitute, diuretics only for congestion. ESC 2018, 2022 AHA/ACC and AHA statement text were not opened. Strength: secondary only.

**E. Dose equivalence and effects.** Loop diuretic: TRANSFORM-HF protocol supports bumetanide 1 mg = furosemide 40 mg and torsemide 1 mg = furosemide 2 mg (the registry arm text also says 2-4 mg), so the commonly quoted 40/20/1 is supported at the 1:2 ratio; no guideline table opened. RALES/EMPHASIS: hyperkalemia event rates verified (EMPHASIS K >5.5 in 11.8% vs 7.2%; RALES pilot K >=5.5 in 13% at 25 mg vs 5% placebo); mean K change values not verified. Spironolactone BP: the dose-ranging RALES study reported significant BP decreases, so "no BP effect" is refuted; the 25 mg magnitude is not verified. Digoxin: the label states it increases LVEF; the DIG abstract reports no LVEF result. Strength: label and trial abstracts primary; mean-change values not verified.
