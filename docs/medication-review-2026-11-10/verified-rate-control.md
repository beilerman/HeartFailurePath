## Answers to the seven required questions

1. **Bradycardia, already on therapy vs starting.**
   - Carvedilol label: "The dose of COREG should be reduced if patients experience bradycardia (heart rate less than 55 beats per minute)" (sec 2.1), repeated in 5.2: if pulse is below 55, reduce the dose. Contraindications are severe bradycardia (unless a permanent pacemaker), sick sinus syndrome, and 2nd/3rd-degree AV block. HR below 55 is a dose-reduction trigger, not a contraindication or a stop rule.
   - Metoprolol succinate label: "If patients experience symptomatic bradycardia, reduce the dose" (HF dosing). Contraindication is severe bradycardia (sinus-node/AV-block wording above). No numeric rate.
   - Bisoprolol (US label, hypertension-only): contraindicated in "marked sinus bradycardia", cardiogenic shock, overt cardiac failure, 2nd/3rd-degree AV block. No numeric rate and no HF dosing in the label.
   - No label gives a numeric heart rate below which an established beta-blocker is stopped, and none gives a numeric start threshold other than "severe/marked bradycardia".
   - ACC ECDP 2021 and ESC 2021 numeric bradycardia advice: NOT VERIFIED (primary text unreachable). The secondary article PMC11911621 says reduce or stop for "bradycardia worsening heart failure" with no number. A web-search snippet attributed "HR below 50 with worsening symptoms: halve the dose" to older (2005-era) practical guidance reproduced by SIGN; I could not read that PDF, so it is unverified.
   - Net: removal at HR <55 is NOT supported. At most the carvedilol label supports a one-step dose reduction at <55; the other two drugs support reduction only for symptomatic bradycardia.
2. **Carvedilol.** Label: start 3.125 mg BID for 2 weeks, then 6.25, 12.5, 25 mg BID at intervals of at least 2 weeks; "a maximum dose of 50 mg twice daily has been administered" to patients weighing over 85 kg (mild-to-moderate HF); COMET used target 25 mg BID. The label does not itself call 50 mg BID a "target". MOCHA (label and PubMed): placebo-corrected LVEF gain was +5, +6, +8 EF units at 6.25, 12.5, 25 mg BID versus +2 for placebo; 25 mg BID was the top MOCHA dose, so there is no dose-response data above 25 BID. Hepatic: contraindicated in "severe hepatic impairment" (label does not name a Child-Pugh class; levels rise 4- to 7-fold in severe cirrhosis).
3. **Carvedilol superiority.** Only COMET: carvedilol versus metoprolol TARTRATE (target 50 mg BID), mortality 34% vs 40%, HR 0.83 (0.74-0.93); composite death-or-admission 0.94 (0.86-1.02), not significant; mean carvedilol dose 42 mg/day (label). No source opened shows superiority over metoprolol succinate or bisoprolol (not an exhaustive literature search, but nothing in what was opened supports it).
4. **Ivabradine.** See rows 12-17. SHIFT QoL: placebo-corrected KCCQ +1.8 (clinical summary) and +2.4 (overall summary); HR reduced 10.1 bpm placebo-corrected at 12 months.
5. **Digoxin.** 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA: Class 2b, LOE B-R; symptomatic HFrEF despite GDMT (or unable to tolerate GDMT); "might be considered" to reduce HF hospitalization. Label: indicated for mild-to-moderate HF and chronic AF rate control; contraindicated only in ventricular fibrillation and hypersensitivity; accessory pathway (WPW) is a Warning 5.1; dosing is by lean body weight and CrCl (table), with 62.5 mcg tablets marketed; label efficacy range: levels under 0.5 ng/mL diminished efficacy, over 2 ng/mL toxicity without benefit; DIG post hoc: 0.5-0.8 ng/mL best, 1.2 or more worse.
6. **Bisoprolol US strengths (single agent).** 5 mg and 10 mg only (Sun label: 5 and 10; PD-Rx label: 10 only). No 1.25 or 2.5 mg single-agent tablet. (The HCTZ combination is a different product.)
7. **Generic ivabradine in US.** Yes. DailyMed lists generic ivabradine SPLs from Novadoz, Northstar, American Health Packaging, Golden State, Ingenus, Bionpharma, Alembic (x2), Zydus (x2) and Camber, besides Corlanor (Amgen).

## Corrections to implement (CONFIRMED / PARTLY CONFIRMED only, most important first)

1. Beta-blockers, bradycardia, patient already on therapy: delete the absolute `pulse < 55` contraindication for continuation. Carvedilol with HR <55, or any BB with symptomatic bradycardia, produces a one-step dose-reduction candidate plus alert. Force removal only for sick sinus syndrome or 2nd/3rd-degree AV block without a pacemaker, or bradycardia persisting at the lowest dose tier.
2. Keep any numeric heart-rate gate for initiation clearly flagged as an engine policy (labels give only "severe/marked bradycardia" as a CI).
3. Carvedilol effects: clamp the dose ratio at 1 (`min(d/25, 1)`); 50 mg BID gets no more credit than 25 mg BID.
4. Ivabradine: add label contraindications (strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, severe hepatic impairment, SSS/SA block/pacemaker dependence, ADHF, clinically significant hypotension or bradycardia) and avoid-warnings for verapamil, diltiazem and CYP3A4 inducers.
5. Ivabradine: HR >=70 criterion applies to initiation only (continuation keeps the +15); add the label HR rules (HR <50 or symptoms: step down 2.5 mg BID; stop at 2.5 mg BID; HR >60: step up to max 7.5 mg BID; 50-60: hold).
6. Ivabradine: add a 2.5 mg BID start for conduction defects or bradycardia-risk patients; eligibility on "maximally tolerated BB" (documented limiting intolerance), not only target dose.
7. Ivabradine KCCQ effect 5 -> about 2 (SHIFT: +1.8 and +2.4 placebo-corrected); HR effect about 10 bpm.
8. Digoxin: add ventricular fibrillation CI; add WPW/accessory-pathway warning; make eGFR<15 and K>5.5 dose-reduction/monitoring rules rather than hard CIs.
9. Digoxin indication: sinus-rhythm HF only after symptoms persist on maximally tolerated GDMT (or GDMT intolerance), Class 2b; AF rate control allowed but ranked below BB optimisation.
10. Digoxin effects: BNP effect 0 (unsupported); HR effect conditional on AF; level target text per DIG (0.5-0.8 men; avoid >=1.2); amiodarone text "reduce about 30-50%".
11. Carvedilol hepatic: contraindicate severe (Child-Pugh C) only; metoprolol hepatic CI becomes a caution.
12. Bisoprolol: flag 1.25/2.5 mg as non-US strengths; note HF use is off-label on the US label.
13. Equalise carvedilol vs succinate/bisoprolol benefit at equipotent target (COMET used tartrate).
14. Ivabradine metadata: `generic_available: true`; fix guideline-class citation to Class 2a (AHA/ACC 2022) and IIa (ESC 2021, secondary).
