## Answers to the eight required questions

1. Strengths: furosemide 20/40/80 (label title); torsemide 5/10/20/100 generic, Soaanz 20/40/60; bumetanide 0.5/1/2; metolazone 2.5/5 (10 mg only mentioned in description); chlorthalidone 25/50 on the label opened (15 mg mentioned elsewhere on it); HCTZ 12.5/25/50. Maximums: furosemide 600 mg/day (label, confirmed); bumetanide 10 mg/day (label, confirmed); torsemide 200 mg/day is supported only as "doses above 200 mg not studied" (label) and an open-access review table; the guideline's own table was not opened, so "guideline max" is NOT VERIFIED.
2. Equivalence and bioavailability: bumetanide 1 mg about furosemide 40 mg (label, confirmed). Torsemide 20 mg about furosemide 40 mg was not found in any opened primary source (NOT VERIFIED); the torsemide label states no equivalence. Bioavailability: furosemide tablet 64% (label), about 50% and variable in reviews; torsemide about 80% (label, CI 75-89%); bumetanide about 80-90% in reviews (label gives no number). IV-to-oral: double furosemide, keep bumetanide and torsemide the same (reviews).
3. Furoscix: see rows above (adults with chronic HF or CKD incl. nephrotic syndrome, no NYHA class, pediatric 43 kg or more; not for chronic use; contraindications anuria, hypersensitivity, adhesives for infusor; 99.6% of IV 80 mg infusor; 30 mg first hour then 12.5 mg/h for 4 h).
4. TRANSFORM-HF: 26.1% vs 26.2%, HR 1.02 (0.89-1.18).
5. CLOROTIC: hydrochlorothiazide; weight loss 2.3 vs 1.5 kg, adjusted difference 1.14 kg; worsening renal function 46.5% vs 17.2%; hypokalemia higher per post hoc (OR 4.90).
6. Metolazone label: HF edema 5-20 mg once daily; electrolyte warnings as above; no intermittent dosing described.
7. Hyperuricemia/gout: see class row; thiazide-type labels say gout can be precipitated, metolazone says it regularly raises uric acid.
8. Low eGFR: higher threshold dose in CKD (open-access reviews; Furoscix and torsemide labels), no number from a guideline I could open.

## Corrections to implement (CONFIRMED / PARTLY CONFIRMED only, most important first)

1. Bumetanide: delete the 4 mg tier (no such tablet; tablets 0.5/1/2 mg) and cap total daily dose at 10 mg in frequency selection (4 mg tid = 12 mg/day exceeds the label maximum).
2. Furoscix: model as a short add-on burst on top of the oral loop (label: not for chronic use), not a standing swap; define doses per burst; price per single-use kit about $822 WAC (Jan 2023) to about $1,450 cash retail (Nov 2026).
3. Furoscix oral-equivalent multiplier 2.0 (infusor is 99.6% of IV; oral furosemide is about 50-64% bioavailable); apply to all effect channels.
4. Furoscix: replace the eGFR<15 contraindication with anuria; keep the adhesive contraindication for the infusor only.
5. CLOROTIC re-anchor: the trial drug is HCTZ; add-on thiazide weight credit about 1.1 kg (adjusted 1.14 kg at 72 h), not 1.75-2.0 kg; add worsening renal function (46.5% vs 17.2%) and hypokalemia (OR 4.9) to thiazide add-on side effects; do not encode "25 mg if eGFR<30" (protocol gives higher HCTZ doses at lower clearance).
6. Torsemide: replace the 50 mg tier with 40 mg (or 60 mg) and add 5 mg; remove or relabel the best-in-class badge (TRANSFORM-HF 26.1% vs 26.2% death, HR 1.02).
7. Furosemide: remove the 160 mg single-tablet tier (or label 2 x 80 mg); compute daily dose (strength x frequency) for the high-dose alert, anchored to the 600 mg/day label ceiling.
8. Apply the gout caution to chlorthalidone, HCTZ and metolazone (labels say gout can be precipitated); add uric acid to their side effects.
9. Metolazone: add 5 mg tier; add hyponatremia and hypokalemia to side effects; record that label HF dosing is 5-20 mg once daily and describes no intermittent schedule, so label any intermittent option as practice-based.
10. HCTZ: add 12.5 mg tier; downgrade eGFR<30 contraindication to caution (CLOROTIC enrolled eGFR as low as 14).
11. Loops at low eGFR: raise threshold/ceiling doses and reduce credit for sub-threshold doses (direction supported; the x0.8 / x0.6 magnitudes are an unsourced assumption).
12. Optional: scale furosemide renal_worsening with daily dose (DOSE: high dose gave transient renal worsening).
