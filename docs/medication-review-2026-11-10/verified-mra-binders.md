## Direct answers to the required questions

1. Eplerenone (INS): contraindicated in all patients with K+ >5.5 at initiation, CrCl <=30 mL/min, or strong CYP3A inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, nefazodone, troleandomycin, clarithromycin, ritonavir, nelfinavir). With a moderate CYP3A inhibitor, do not exceed 25 mg once daily (HF).
2. Kerendia (KER, includes HF indication, LVEF >=40%): contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and adrenal insufficiency. Start 20 mg if eGFR >=60, 10 mg if eGFR >=25 to <60, not recommended below 25. HF target 40 mg (eGFR >=60) or 20 mg (eGFR >=25 to <60). Do not start if K+ >5.0. HF titration, hold and restart rules are in item 14.
3. Guideline eGFR >30 / K+ <5.0: not confirmed from the primary guideline. Aldactone: start only if K+ <=5.0 and eGFR >50; if hyperkalemia on 25 mg daily go to 25 mg every other day; label gives no numeric K+ rule beyond that ("decrease the dose or discontinue"). Eplerenone: see item 30.
4. Mean K+ rise in HF trials: not found in opened sources. Incidence: RALES serious hyperkalemia minimal in both arms; EPHESUS K+ >5.5 15.6% vs 11.2%, >=6.0 5.5% vs 3.9%; EMPHASIS-HF K+ >5.5 11.8% vs 7.2%.
5. BP, spironolactone vs eplerenone at HF doses: no head-to-head HF data in opened sources. INS gives only hypertension-trial effects (6-13 / 3-7 mmHg placebo-subtracted at 50-200 mg). FINEARTS-HF: SBP <100 mmHg in 18.5% vs 12.4% (ACC article).
6. Lokelma: sodium about 400 mg per 5 g; edema 4.4/5.9/16.1% at 5/10/15 g (placebo 2.4%); dosing as in item 26; maintenance K+ 4.8/4.5/4.4 vs 5.1 placebo. Veltassa: start 8.4 g, max 25.2 g, hypomagnesemia 5.3%, 3-hour separation.
7. DIAMOND: primary endpoint was K+ change (+0.03 patiromer vs +0.13 placebo, difference -0.10 mmol/L); secondary results favored patiromer for K+ >5.5 events and MRA dose reductions; no hard-outcome benefit reported.
8. Prices: see items 32-34.

## Corrections to implement (CONFIRMED / PARTLY CONFIRMED only, most important first)

1. Eplerenone contraindication: strong CYP3A inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, nefazodone, troleandomycin, clarithromycin, ritonavir, nelfinavir); cap at 25 mg once daily with a moderate CYP3A inhibitor; contraindicate at CrCl/eGFR <=30 (not <30).
2. Finerenone contraindications: strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and adrenal insufficiency; monitoring warning for moderate/weak inhibitors (erythromycin, verapamil).
3. New-start K+ gate for all MRAs: K+ >5.0 excludes initiation (spironolactone and finerenone labels; eplerenone label allows 5.5, so 5.0 is a stricter policy choice).
4. Continuation rule instead of force-removal at K+ >5.5: K+ <5.5 maintain; 5.5-5.9 reduce one dose step (finerenone 10 mg and eplerenone 25 mg every other day: withhold); >=6.0 withhold; restart finerenone at 10 mg and eplerenone at 25 mg every other day once K+ <5.5.
5. Finerenone HF target: 20 mg when eGFR >=25 to <60, 40 mg when eGFR >=60; start 10 mg (eGFR <60) or 20 mg (eGFR >=60); do not initiate if eGFR <25; progress to 40 mg only if K+ <5.0 and eGFR was >=60 at initiation.
6. Spironolactone label start rule: K+ <=5.0 and eGFR >50 for 25 mg daily; eGFR 30-50 start 25 mg every other day; add Addison's disease as a contraindication; correct the "Category X" comment (no letter category on the label).
7. Lokelma: edema by dose (4.4/5.9/16.1% at 5/10/15 g); about 400 mg sodium per 5 g (800 mg per 10 g); edema/diuretic warning for every HF patient; GI-obstruction caution; 2-hour separation warning; add 15 g tier.
8. Lokelma K+ effect for prevention/maintenance: 5 g -0.3, 10 g -0.6 (15 g -0.7), placebo-adjusted; do not project K+ below about 4.5.
9. Patiromer: add 16.8 g and 25.2 g tiers; 3-hour separation warning; Mg monitoring prompt; GI-motility caution; DIAMOND-style prevention effect about -0.1 mmol/L.
10. Binder "+50" special feature: require an MRA/RAASi in the regimen (DIAMOND supports enabling MRA dosing, not hard outcomes); point size is a judgement call.
11. Gynecomastia: scale spironolactone rate with dose (about 9-10% around 25 mg in RALES).
12. Finerenone KCCQ credit: lower toward about 1.6 points once confirmed from the FINEARTS-HF paper.
13. Prices: Kerendia cash about $900; Lokelma cash about $900.
