# Verification of estimates-foundational.json (68 rows)

Each row was re-checked against the source by fetching the PubMed abstract (efetch) or open full text (PMC page) and searching for the quote text and numbers. Quotes in 63 of the 68 rows were found verbatim in the fetched text. The other 5: three CIBIS-II hospitalization rows (not in the abstract; not found anywhere), and the two Cleland 2018 LVEF >=50 rows (checked directly against Table 2 on the PMC page, values match).

Totals: CONFIRMED 48, CORRECTED 16, NOT CONFIRMED 4 (sum 68).

Convention: CORRECTED means the stored number or its outcome definition does not match what the row says it is (recurrent-event ratio where a first-event figure is quotable, a proxy outcome, a stale assumed-neutral, or a mismatched population). Rows that are accurate but are replaced in resolved-foundational.json only because the class shares one estimate are marked CONFIRMED.
